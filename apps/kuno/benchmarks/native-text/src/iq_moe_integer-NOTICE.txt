The explicit native Rust-emitted WGSL IQ integer-dot operator uses original
GGML IQ3_XXS/IQ4_NL/IQ4_XS packing and codebooks from llama.cpp tag b11232.
The independent C fixture source/hashes are recorded in
crates/kuno-text/tests/fixtures/iq-b11232/receipt.json. No C/CUDA engine is linked.

Its original-code integer factorization was studied against Strata revision
fb58e0dbc8399662c0e47c76578c6e878b14f6cf,
src/prefill/moe_fused_iq.cu (load_unit/convert) and
src/kernels/cuda/native_mmvq.cu (iq4_xs_q8_dot), which adapts llama.cpp
3cf03257f219afbe7334045ff7c6a06ac68c627d. Strata uses native CUDA/HIP matrix
instructions, not this WGSL implementation. No host-mapped doorbell, device
reset, route prediction or source paging mechanism is copied here.

Two residual Q8 activation planes build on Kuno's existing explicit
next_moe_integer.wgsl experiment. This changes activation precision and F32
reduction/scale order. It does not replace the ordinary original64 F32 policy
or establish original model quality, private inference, or speed acceptance.
